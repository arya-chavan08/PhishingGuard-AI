Cyber Law & Digital Offences

Understand important cyber-law concepts, common digital offences and responsible cybercrime reporting in India.

📜

Information Technology Act

The Information Technology Act, 2000 is an important Indian law dealing with electronic records, electronic communication and several computer-related offences.

Key Point

Cyber offences may have legal consequences under applicable laws depending on the facts of a case.

🪪

Identity Theft

Section 66C of the Information Technology Act addresses fraudulent or dishonest use of another person's electronic signature, password or other unique identification feature.

Section 66C

Identity theft involving specified digital identification features can constitute an offence under the Act.

🎭

Online Personation

Section 66D addresses cheating by personation using a communication device or computer resource.

Section 66D

Online impersonation used for cheating can fall within this provision.

🔒

Privacy Protection

Section 66E of the Information Technology Act deals with certain intentional or knowing acts involving capture, publication or transmission of images of private areas without consent in circumstances covered by the section.

Privacy

Digital privacy should be respected and personal information should be handled responsibly.

🚨

Cybercrime Reporting

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal allows victims and complainants to report cybercrime complaints online.

Official Helpline

Cyber financial fraud can be reported immediately through the national helpline 1930.

🛡️

Digital Responsibility

Responsible digital behaviour helps reduce cybercrime risks and supports safer use of online services.

  • Protect passwords and authentication information
  • Verify unexpected online requests
  • Do not impersonate others online
  • Respect privacy and digital information
OFFICIAL CYBERCRIME REPORTING

For cybercrime complaints in India, use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or the appropriate law-enforcement channel.

Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
